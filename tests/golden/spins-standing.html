Part I Standing instructions

1 General

1.1 Purpose. These fictional instructions demonstrate a numbered SPINS publication. They are not real-world flight procedures.

1.2 Applicability. These standing instructions apply to the exercise participants. Mission-period changes identify the baseline revision they amend.

Note 1. Paragraph numbers and cross-references update automatically when instructions are inserted, moved, or removed.

2.2 Reports. Report the change in status using the published call sequence.

2.2.1 Use the assigned mission number when identifying the task.

2.2.1.1 If a report requires clarification, retain the original report context.

2.2.1.1.1 Clearly identify the corrected item.

2.2.1.1.1.1 A correction does not change unrelated instructions.

3.2 Restrictions

3.2.1 Area entry. Obtain exercise-control clearance before entering an area for which clearance is required. Apply the exceptions and limitations in the assigned mission briefing.

3.2.2

  1. Identify the requested area and intended activity.
  2. Read back the clearance and its limits.
  3. Report clear of the area when the assigned activity is complete.

4 Airfield procedures

4.1 NORTH FIELD

4.1.1 Departure. Follow local control instructions and the departure route assigned in the flight briefing.

4.1.2 Entry into controlled exercise airspace. Local departure clearance does not replace the area-entry procedure in Part I, Paragraph 3.2.1.

4.1.3 Recovery. Coordinate the arrival with local control. Use the recovery and alternate instructions in the flight briefing.
