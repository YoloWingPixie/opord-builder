AirDefenseAppendix

An appendix for air and missile defence is Appendix 7 (Air and Missile Defense) of Annex D. FM 6-0 (2022) puts these data in Annex D. FM 5-0 (2024) puts them in a different annex, Annex I, which openAIX does not use.

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
defended_assetslist of DefendedAssetThis list contains the defended assets, in order.

measureslist of IdentifierThis list identifies the control measures that are air defence measures.
warningslist of AirDefenseWarningThis list contains the air defence warnings.

AirSupportAppendix

An air support appendix is Appendix 5 (Air Support) of Annex D.

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
agencieslist of IdentifierThis list identifies the command and control agencies that control air support.
ato_missionslist of IdentifierThis list identifies the missions of the air tasking order (ATO) that give air interdiction and air reconnaissance. The orders field identifies the ATO.
cas_allocationslist of CasAllocationThis list contains the close air support (CAS) allocations.
cas_briefslist of CasBriefThis list contains the close air support (CAS) briefs that the staff writes before the mission. Each brief has the 9-line data of JP 3-09.3.
pointslist of IdentifierThis list identifies the points that are contact points, initial points and holding points for air support. Each point shall have the role control or initial or marshalling.
